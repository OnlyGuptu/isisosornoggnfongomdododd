1. Find the degrees of the following polynomials:

(i) 2x² - 5x + 3      (ii) y³ + 2y - 1      (iii) -9      (iv) 4z - 3

  • (i) 2x² - 5x + 3: The highest power of the variable x is 2, so the degree is 2.
  • (ii) y³ + 2y - 1: The highest power of the variable y is 3, so the degree is 3.
  • (iii) -9: This is a non-zero constant polynomial (can be written as -9x⁰). Its degree is 0.
  • (iv) 4z - 3: The highest power of the variable z is 1, so the degree is 1.

3. The present age of Salil's mother is three times Salil's present age. After 5 years, their ages will add up to 70 years. Find their present ages.

  3. After 5 years, Salil's age will be x + 5, and his mother's age will be 3x + 5.

    4x = 60
    x = 15

Exercise Set 2.5

1. A learning platform charges a fixed monthly fee and an additional cost per digital learning module accessed. A student observes that when she accessed 10 modules, her bill was ₹400. When she accessed 14 modules, her bill was ₹500. If the monthly bill y depends on the number of modules accessed, x, according to the relation y = ax + b, find the values of a and b.

  1. We have two equations based on the information provided:
    400 = 10a + b
    500 = 14a + b
  2. Subtract the first equation from the second to eliminate b:
    (500 - 400) = (14a + b) - (10a + b)
    100 = 4a
    a = 25
  3. Substitute a = 25 back into the first equation to find b:
    400 = 10(25) + b
    400 = 250 + b
    b = 150
  4. The relationship is y = 25x + 150.

If the monthly bill y depends on the hours of the use of the badminton court, x, according to the relation y = ax + b, find the values of a and b.

    1100 = 15a + b

    300 = 5a

  4. The relationship is y = 60x + 200.

Exercise Set 2.5

3. Consider the relationship between temperature measured in degrees Celsius (°C) and degrees Fahrenheit (°F), which is given by °C = a°F + b. Find a and b, given that ice melts at 0 degrees Celsius and 32 degrees Fahrenheit, and water boils at 100 degrees Celsius and 212 degrees Fahrenheit.

  1. Set up the equations based on the given points:
    0 = 32a + b
    100 = 212a + b
  2. Subtract the first equation from the second:
    100 = 180a
    a = 100 / 180 = 5/9
  3. Substitute a = 5/9 into the first equation:
    0 = 32(5/9) + b
    b = -160/9
  4. The relationship is °C = (5/9)°F - 160/9 (or °C = (5/9)(°F - 32)).

Exercise Set 2.6

1. Draw the graphs of the following sets of lines. In each case, reflect on the role of 'a' and 'b'.

(i) y = 3x - 1, y = 3x, y = 3x + 1

(ii) y = 4x, y = 2x, y = x

(iii) y = -6x, y = -3x, y = -x

(iv) y = 5x, y = -5x

(v) y = -2x - 3, y = -2x, y = 2x + 3

Reflections on the parameters 'a' (slope) and 'b' (y-intercept):

  • (i) y = 3x - 1, y = 3x, y = 3x + 1: Here, the slope a is constant (3), so the three lines are parallel. The y-intercept b changes (-1, 0, 1), shifting the parallel lines vertically up and down the y-axis.
  • (ii) y = 4x, y = 2x, y = x: Here, the y-intercept b is fixed at 0 (all pass through the origin). The slope a changes (4, 2, 1). As a increases, the lines become progressively steeper (closer to the y-axis).
  • (iii) y = -6x, y = -3x, y = -x: All pass through the origin. Since slopes are negative, they represent linear decay. As the magnitude of a increases (from 1 to 6), the lines get steeper in the negative direction.
  • (iv) y = 5x, y = -5x: Both pass through the origin. They have the same steepness but opposite directions; y = 5x represents growth (rising left to right), while y = -5x represents decay (falling left to right). They are reflections of each other across the y-axis.
  • (v) y = -2x - 3, y = -2x, y = 2x + 3: The first two lines (y = -2x - 3 and y = -2x) are parallel to each other with a negative slope, but with different y-intercepts (-3 and 0). The third line (y = 2x + 3) has a positive slope (intersecting the others) and cuts the y-axis at +3.
Role of 'b': Parallel lines with same slope (a = 3) shifted vertically by y-intercept b

6. The digits of a two-digit number differ by 3. If the digits are interchanged, and the resulting number is added to the original number, we get 143. Find both the numbers.

  1. Let the tens digit be t and the units digit be u. The original number is 10t + u.
  2. The interchanged number is 10u + t.
  3. Their sum is 143:
    (10t + u) + (10u + t) = 143
    11t + 11u = 143
    t + u = 13
  4. We are also given that the digits differ by 3, so either t - u = 3 or u - t = 3.
  5. Case 1: t - u = 3
    Adding the equations: (t + u) + (t - u) = 13 + 3 ⇒ 2t = 16 ⇒ t = 8
    If t = 8, then u = 5. The number is 85.
  6. Case 2: u - t = 3
    Adding the equations: (u + t) + (u - t) = 13 + 3 ⇒ 2u = 16 ⇒ u = 8
    If u = 8, then t = 5. The number is 58.
  7. The two possible numbers are 58 and 85.

8. If the temperature of a liquid can be measured in Kelvin units as x K and in Fahrenheit units as y °F, the relation between the two systems of measurement of temperature is given by the linear equation y = 9/5(x - 273) + 32.

(i) Find the temperature of the liquid in Fahrenheit if the temperature of the liquid is 313 K.

(ii) If the temperature is 158 °F. then find the temperature in Kelvin.

    Substitute x = 313 into the equation:
    y = 9/5(313 - 273) + 32
    y = 9/5(40) + 32
    y = 9(8) + 32 = 72 + 32 = 104 °F
  • (ii) From Fahrenheit to Kelvin:
    Substitute y = 158 into the equation:
    158 = 9/5(x - 273) + 32
    126 = 9/5(x - 273)
    126 × (5/9) = x - 273
    70 = x - 273
    x = 70 + 273 = 343 K

10. The graph of a linear polynomial p(x) passes through the points (1, 5) and (3, 11).

(i) Find the polynomial p(x).

(ii) Find the coordinates where the graph of p(x) cuts the axes.

End-of-Chapter Exercises

11. Let p(x) = ax + b and q(x) = cx + d be two linear polynomials such that:

(i) p(0) = 5.

(ii) The polynomial p(x) - q(x) cuts the x-axis at (3, 0).

(iii) The sum p(x) + q(x) is equal to 6x + 4 for all real x.

Find the polynomials p(x) and q(x).

  1. From (i), p(0) = 5 ⇒ a(0) + b = 5 ⇒ b = 5.
  2. From (iii), p(x) + q(x) = (a + c)x + (b + d) = 6x + 4.
    This means a + c = 6 and b + d = 4.
    Since b = 5, then 5 + d = 4 ⇒ d = -1.
  3. From (ii), p(3) - q(3) = 0 ⇒ p(3) = q(3).
    3a + b = 3c + d
    Substitute b = 5 and d = -1:
    3a + 5 = 3c - 1 ⇒ 3a - 3c = -6 ⇒ a - c = -2.
  4. We now have a system for a and c:
    a + c = 6
    a - c = -2
    Adding them: 2a = 4 ⇒ a = 2.
    Substituting back: 2 + c = 6 ⇒ c = 4.
  5. The polynomials are: p(x) = 2x + 5 and q(x) = 4x - 1.

13. Let p(x) = ax + b and q(x) = cx + d be two linear polynomials such that:

(i) The graph of p(x) passes through the points (2, 3) and (6, 11).

(ii) The graph of q(x) passes through the point (4, -1).

(iii) The graph of q(x) is parallel to the graph of p(x).

Find the polynomials p(x) and q(x). Also, find the coordinates of the point where these lines meet the x-axis.

  1. Find p(x):
    3 = 2a + b
    11 = 6a + b
    Subtracting gives 4a = 8 ⇒ a = 2. Substituting back gives b = -1.
    So, p(x) = 2x - 1.
  2. Find q(x):
    Because q(x) is parallel to p(x), its slope c must equal a. So, c = 2.
    q(x) = 2x + d. It passes through (4, -1), so:
    -1 = 2(4) + d ⇒ -1 = 8 + d ⇒ d = -9.
    So, q(x) = 2x - 9.
  3. X-axis intercepts (where y = 0):
    For p(x): 2x - 1 = 0 ⇒ x = 1/2. Coordinates: (1/2, 0).
    For q(x): 2x - 9 = 0 ⇒ x = 9/2. Coordinates: (9/2, 0).

14. What do all linear functions of the form f(x) = ax + a, a > 0, have in common?

Given f(x) = ax + a, where a > 0. We can factor this as f(x) = a(x + 1).

  1. Positive Slope: The slope is a. Since a > 0, all lines have a positive slope and rise from left to right (linear growth).
  2. Equal Slope and Y-intercept: When x = 0, f(0) = a. The y-intercept is (0, a), which is above the origin since a > 0.
  3. Common X-intercept: To find where the line cuts the x-axis, set f(x) = 0:
    a(x + 1) = 0
    Since a ≠ 0, we have x + 1 = 0 ⇒ x = -1.
    Therefore, all such lines pass through the identical fixed point (-1, 0) on the x-axis.
Pencil of Lines: Every member of f(x) = a(x + 1) pivots through (−1, 0) with slope a
