Q1

Based on the IUCN Red List stacked column chart:
(i) What does 14,234 represent?
(ii) Approx how many reptile species in 2016?
(iii) Which class(es) had a small increase between 2007 and 2019?

Stacked Column Chart: Number of animal species in the IUCN Red List by class (2007–2019)

Answer:
(i) It represents the total cumulative number of endangered animal species (across all listed classes) in the year 2019.
(ii) Looking at the maroon bar segment for 2016, it spans roughly from 1,000 to 2,000 on the y-axis, indicating approximately 1,000 reptile species.
(iii) 'Mammals' (bottom dark blue bar) and 'Birds' (light blue bar) show relatively small increases over that time period.

About NCERTSolved

Student-First Pedagogical Hub • Editorial Standards • Academic Integrity

1. Our Educational Mission

NCERTSolved was founded by students and educators with a singular goal: to provide high-school students with crystal-clear, verified, and accessible textbook solutions. Traditional educational portals often conceal answers behind intrusive paywalls, popups, or low-quality automated scans. We deliver clean, step-by-step textbook solutions accompanied by interactive mathematical formulas, labelled diagrams, and conceptual hints designed to build genuine conceptual mastery.

2. Rigorous Editorial & Verification Methodology (E-E-A-T)

Every single solution published on NCERTSolved undergoes a multi-tier academic review process:

  • Formula & Proof Verification: Mathematical solutions (Ganita Prakash / Ganita Manjari) are verified step-by-step using both algebraic proof methods and Baudhāyana-Pythagorean geometric verification.
  • Scientific Accuracy: Science solutions (Physics, Chemistry, and Biology) are vetted against official CBSE/NCERT curriculum benchmarks to ensure proper SI units, balanced chemical equations, and accurate biological nomenclature.
  • Original Pedagogical Hints: Rather than mere answer keys, exercises feature dedicated "💡 Show Hint / Formula" callouts to foster problem-solving intuition.
